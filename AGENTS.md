# Working together on AMR ROS 2

Read [the project plan](docs/roadmap.md) before starting a feature.

- Work on one feature at a time. At the start, explain where it fits in the robot, what goes in and comes out, and what working behavior we’ll verify. Give the project owner a chance to shape the approach before implementation.
- Implement in small steps. Explain consequential choices and show the commands, ROS 2 topics, or observations the project owner can use to follow the work. Let the owner drive hands-on steps when they want to.
- Close each feature with a walkthrough of what changed, what we observed, and anything still unverified. Wait for the project owner to choose or approve the next feature before starting it.
- Update the roadmap when a milestone is verified or an architecture decision changes.
